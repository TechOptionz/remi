About Remi

Twenty-three years of building, leading and writing down what actually works.

Remi has founded and run companies, trained thousands of leaders and written books that have reached millions. The frames on this site were built in those rooms, not in theory.

Today she works at the strategic level: facilitating leadership teams, speaking to large audiences and publishing the models. She is not available for individual coaching.
