About Remi

Author · Founder · Keynote speaker

Remi is a business leader, strategist and speaker who has built companies, trained thousands of leaders and written books that have reached millions. Her work sits where leadership, psychology and strategy meet.

She is known for original frameworks that make difficult things visible: the level of thinking in a room, the reason capable people stay stuck, and what it takes to lead without carrying everyone.

Remi works with founders, executives and teams who want straight answers and lasting change, not motivation.
