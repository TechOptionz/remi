Home/About Remi

Hi, I'm Remi

In 2003, I wasn't searching for a new career.

I was depressed, unwell and thinking about suicide. At that point, my greatest ambition was not to build a business, write books or teach thousands of people. It was to experience one whole day without anxiety.

I needed to answer one burning question:

Why is it that some people, even though they experience heartbreak, triumph?

Why are some people defined by what happened to them, while others somehow find a way to become more fully themselves?

I began training as a coach because I needed to understand.

If you are struggling right now, please reach out. In Australia, Lifeline is available 24 hours a day on 13 11 14.

That question became my life's work

It led me into coaching, psychology, human behaviour, attachment theory, trauma, relationships, parts work and the emotional architecture beneath the patterns we repeat.

It led me into thousands of hours holding space for people when the explanation they had for their pain was no longer enough.

And, slowly, it helped me find my own way home.

Everything else came later.

The business. The books. The models. The stages. The awards. The millions of people reached.

They all grew from one woman trying to understand why some people experience heartbreak and still find a way to triumph.

More than twenty-three years later, I'm still following that question.

The Foundation

Success was always meant to travel further than me

As The Coaching Institute grew, I became increasingly aware that the opportunity to pursue a meaningful life was not equally available to everyone. So I founded The Coaching Institute Foundation.

Through my personal giving, the Foundation and the generosity of our community, we supported causes that were confronting, overlooked or unlikely to attract fashionable attention — Animals Asia, Moon Bear Rescue, and people and communities experiencing profound hardship.

If we had the ability to help, then we had a responsibility to notice where help was needed.
