Meet the T.R.U.S.T.M.E. model

People do not all make sense of the world from the same place. T.R.U.S.T.M.E. reveals the level of thinking shaping what a person, group or organisation notices, values, fears and can take responsibility for.

Under pressure, our natural level becomes easier to see.

  1. 7EvolutionWhat serves the whole now?
  2. 6MeaningWhat are we contributing beyond ourselves?
  3. 5TenacityHow do we improve and achieve?
  4. 4SystemsHow do we make this stable and repeatable?
  5. 3UniquenessWho am I when I stop conforming?
  6. 2RelatabilityWhere do I belong and whose truth do I trust?
  7. 1TrustCan I survive and am I enough?

You cannot lead everyone from the same place

The level of thinking present determines what kind of leadership can actually be heard.

You cannot skip a level

Each stage has problems that must be solved before the next becomes available.

Every level can be functional or dysfunctional

Belonging can create trust or conformity. Systems can create freedom or bureaucracy.

The thinking that created the problem cannot solve it

The leader must respond from the level the situation requires.

When you stop carrying

You do not abandon people. You stop doing the developmental work that belongs to them.

Clarify accountability

Make ownership visible before stepping in.

Match the role to the readiness

Do not confuse potential with current capability.

Build systems that hold

Replace memory, heroics and rescue with repeatability.

Let the best person lead

At Evolution, function matters more than status.
